Let $d(n)$ denote the number of positive divisors of a positive integer $n$. If $k$ is a given odd positive integer, prove that there exists an increasing arithmetic progression of positive integers $(a_1, a_2, \dots, a_{2019})$ such that
\[
\gcd\bigl(k,\, d(a_1)d(a_2)\cdots d(a_{2019})\bigr) = 1.
\]
