Use Dirichlet’s theorem to produce large primes $p \equiv 1 \pmod{S}$ where $S$ is the product of primes less than 2014. Force $n \equiv -c \pmod{p}$ and $n \equiv 1 \pmod{p-1}$ so that Fermat’s Little Theorem reduces the divisibility condition to a simple algebraic relation between $a$, $b$ and $c$. Then vary the residue of $n$ modulo $p-1$ to obtain further relations that pin down the only possible triple.
