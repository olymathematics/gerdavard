For each positive integer $n$, denote by $\omega(n)$ the number of distinct prime divisors of $n$ (for example, $\omega(1)=0$ and $\omega(12)=2$). Find all polynomials $P(x)$ with integer coefficients such that whenever $n$ is a positive integer satisfying $\omega(n)>2023^{2023}$, then $P(n)$ is also a positive integer and
$$
\omega(n)\geq\omega(P(n)).
$$
