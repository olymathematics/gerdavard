Let $d$ be a common divisor of $21n+4$ and $14n+3$. Then $d$ divides
\[
    2(21n+4)=42n+8
    \qquad\text{and}\qquad
    3(14n+3)=42n+9.
\]
Therefore, $d$ divides their difference,
\[
    (42n+9)-(42n+8)=1.
\]
Thus $d=1$, so the numerator and denominator are relatively prime. Hence
$\dfrac{21n+4}{14n+3}$ is irreducible for every natural number $n$.
